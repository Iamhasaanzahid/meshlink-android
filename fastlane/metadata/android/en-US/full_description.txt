MeshLink is a free messenger and calling app that works without internet.

Messages and calls travel directly between phones over the phone's own radios:

* Wi-Fi Direct (phone-to-phone Wi-Fi, no router)
* Bluetooth LE
* Cellular radio (SMS, no data needed)

Multi-hop forwarding lets a message travel from phone to phone when no tower or hotspot is available.

Features:

* WhatsApp-style chat list, conversations, calls, contacts and settings
* Message delivery status and tap-to-retry
* Free voice calls over Wi-Fi Direct with ring, accept, mute, speaker and hang up, plus a call log
* A normal SIM call fallback when the phones are not paired
* Identity created on the device; no server, no account, no tracking

All chats and call history are stored locally on the phone.
